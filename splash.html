Features

Everything You Need

Powerful steganography tools built for simplicity and security

🖼️

Image Steganography

Hide secret messages within PNG and JPEG images using LSB (Least Significant Bit) encoding. The image looks identical to the naked eye.

📄

PDF Steganography

Embed hidden messages inside PDF files. The document remains fully readable and functional — your secret data rides along invisibly.

🔑

Auto-Generated Keys

Unique encryption keys are auto-generated for every encoding session. No weak passwords — just strong, random 16-character keys.

📧

Gmail Integration

Share your secret key via Gmail with one click. The compose window opens with the key and decoding instructions pre-filled.

🌐

Fully Offline

Runs 100% in your browser. No server, no uploads, no tracking. Your files and messages never leave your device.

⚡

Instant Processing

Encode and decode in milliseconds. No waiting for server responses — everything happens locally at native speed.

Process

How It Works

Three simple steps to hide and reveal secret messages

01

Upload & Write

Choose a cover image or PDF, then type your secret message. The tool calculates how much data your file can hold.

02

Encode & Download

Click Encode — a unique key is auto-generated and your message is hidden inside the file. Download the encoded file instantly.

03

Share & Decode

Send the encoded file and key to your recipient. They upload the file, enter the key, and the hidden message is revealed.
